\section*{Verdicts and finding categories used throughout}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{3.3cm}p{11.9cm}@{}}
permitted & The probed call reached the kernel and was not refused, or returned the error its oracle guarantees\\
denied & The call was refused before reaching the oracle's point (an errno other than the guaranteed one)\\
unimplemented & The kernel of this cell does not provide the entry point (declared absence errno)\\
killed & The probe's child was terminated by SIGSYS, the outcome of a seccomp kill action\\
timed-out & The probe exceeded its risk-class timeout and was killed by the engine\\
not-applicable & The entry point does not exist on this architecture; decided before anything runs\\
expectation violation & Reachable, and the reference posture asserts it should not be\\
claim violation & The manifest asserts a protection that the measurement contradicts\\
undeclared exposure & Reachable, and neither the manifest nor the posture addresses it\\
\end{longtable}
